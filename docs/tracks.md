PS-01 · Track 1

AI Media Pipelines

Build a workflow that automatically ingests, organizes, analyzes, optimizes, or delivers media using Cloudinary's AI Skills Pack.

What this means in plain English: build something where media goes in, Cloudinary's AI does something smart to it automatically, like auto-tagging what is in a photo, removing backgrounds, cropping to the right part of the image, or moderating content, and the output is useful.

Capabilities to use: Upload API, auto-tagging, background removal, content-aware cropping, moderation, image and video transformations, Search API, structured metadata, optimized delivery with f_auto and q_auto.

Good example ideas: An automatic content moderation tool for user-generated images. A smart photo organizer that tags and categorizes uploads automatically. A video processing pipeline that optimizes and delivers clips at the right format and quality.
PS-02 · Track 2
6 teams
Generative Content Workflows

Use Cloudinary's AI image generation workflow, including variations and model choice, inside an existing pipeline.

What this means in plain English: build something where AI generates or transforms images, and those images flow through a Cloudinary pipeline to be delivered, stored, or used in a product.

Capabilities to use: Cloudinary AI image generation, generative variations, model choice, AI Vision, upload and delivery APIs, image transformations.

Good example ideas: A product photography tool that generates multiple variants of a product image automatically. A marketing asset generator that creates social media visuals from a brief. An avatar or character creation tool for a game or app.
PS-03 · Track 3
6 teams
Your Media-Savvy Startup

Build a startup-style product where media is genuinely central to the user experience. This is an open track.

What this means in plain English: build an actual product, something you could pitch as a startup, where Cloudinary is doing real work managing, delivering, or transforming the media that the product depends on.

Capabilities to use: Any meaningful combination of media upload, management, transformation, optimization, video, audio, search, tagging, structured metadata, AI, or delivery.

Good example ideas: A marketplace where sellers upload product photos and Cloudinary automatically optimizes them. A creator platform where user videos are stored, transformed, and delivered by Cloudinary. An ed-tech tool where course thumbnails and media are managed through Cloudinary.
